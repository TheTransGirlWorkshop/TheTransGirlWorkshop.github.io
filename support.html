Frequently asked questions

Where do I download a game I own?

Open Workshop Launcher, sign in with the Workshop account you used for the purchase and open Library.

How do I recover access to my account?

Email Workshop support with the email address on your Workshop account. Keep your password and payment details private.

Where can I ask about a purchase or refund?

Contact support with your product name and order reference. For purchases made on a different marketplace, use that marketplace’s order and refund tools.
