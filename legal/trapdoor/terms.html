Terms of Service

Last updated: 5 October 2026

Purpose

TrapDoor is an automated Discord moderation utility operated by The Trans Girl Workshop. Its primary feature is a honeypot channel that can automatically ban non-exempt members who send a message in that channel.

Administrator responsibility

Server owners and administrators are responsible for choosing whether to enable TrapDoor, making its warning reasonably visible, configuring exemptions, assigning appropriate bot permissions, reviewing moderation logs and using the service in accordance with Discord's rules and applicable law.

Automated moderation

The honeypot is intentionally strict. A non-exempt account that posts in the configured trap channel may be automatically banned. Administrators should test configuration and exemptions before relying on TrapDoor in a production community.

Permissions and role hierarchy

TrapDoor can only perform actions Discord permits. Server administrators are responsible for placing the bot's role appropriately and granting only the permissions required for their intended use.

Availability

TrapDoor is provided without a guarantee of uninterrupted availability. Features, hosting arrangements and behaviour may change as Discord's API, policies or technical requirements change.

Acceptable use

TrapDoor may not be used to circumvent Discord safety systems, harvest credentials, impersonate users, facilitate abuse or perform activity that violates Discord's rules or applicable law.
