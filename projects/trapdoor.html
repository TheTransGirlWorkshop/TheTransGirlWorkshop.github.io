Discord moderation

TrapDoor

A lightweight anti-bot honeypot built to catch accounts that blindly spam available Discord channels.

Add TrapDoor to DiscordSupport development

How it works

TrapDoor creates a clearly warned honeypot channel. Legitimate members leave it untouched. A non-exempt account that sends a message in that channel is automatically banned and the action is recorded in a private moderation log.

Privacy-conscious by design

TrapDoor does not require Discord's Message Content privileged intent and does not persist message content in its own database. It reacts to the existence of a message in the configured honeypot channel.

Commands

/honeypot setup/honeypot status/honeypot enable/honeypot disable/honeypot warning/honeypot test/honeypot exempt-add/honeypot exempt-remove/honeypot exempt-list/support/about
Safe self-test

/honeypot test verifies configuration, channel access, permissions, logging, role hierarchy and warning-message management without banning a member.

Automatic exemptions

The server owner, bots, webhooks, members with Administrator, members with Ban Members, and explicitly configured exempt roles are ignored.
