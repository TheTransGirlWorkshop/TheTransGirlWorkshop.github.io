TrapDoor

A Discord honeypot bot that catches spam bots and bad actors in a clearly marked trap channel. When a non-exempt account posts there, TrapDoor automatically bans it and records the action in a private moderation log.

Add TrapDoor to DiscordGet support

How it works

TrapDoor creates a clearly warned honeypot channel. A non-exempt account that posts in it is automatically banned and the action is recorded in a private moderation log.

TrapDoor runs without Discord’s Message Content privileged intent. It reacts to the presence of a message in the configured channel and stores no message content in its own database.

Commands

  • /honeypot setup
  • /honeypot status
  • /honeypot enable
  • /honeypot disable
  • /honeypot warning
  • /honeypot test
  • /honeypot exempt-add
  • /honeypot exempt-remove
  • /honeypot exempt-list
  • /support
  • /about

/honeypot test checks configuration, channel access, permissions, logging, role hierarchy and warning-message management without banning a member.

Automatic exemptions

The server owner, bots, webhooks, members with Administrator or Ban Members, and explicitly exempt roles are ignored.
